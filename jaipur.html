Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur, the "Pink City", is a UNESCO World Heritage city known for its grand forts, palaces, vibrant bazaars, and royal heritage. A must-visit destination in Rajasthan.

Highlights

Forts & Palaces

Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jal Mahal.

Bazaars

Johari Bazaar, Bapu Bazaar – perfect for shopping.

Culture

Rajasthani folk music, dance, and handicrafts.

Desert Tours

Excursions to sand dunes & camel rides nearby.

Travel Information

  • Best Time: October – March
  • Nearest Airport: Jaipur International Airport (JAI)
  • Top Hotels: Rambagh Palace, ITC Rajputana, Samode Haveli
  • Local Transport: Auto-rickshaws, Taxis, Metro, Cabs
