Welcome to Kerala

Known as "God’s Own Country", Kerala is famous for its backwaters, lush tea plantations, houseboats, and serene beaches. It’s the perfect mix of nature, culture, and relaxation.

Kerala Highlights

Highlights

Backwaters

Houseboat cruises in Alleppey and Kumarakom.

Hills

Tea plantations and cool climate in Munnar.

Ayurveda

Authentic spa and wellness treatments.

Beaches

Kovalam, Varkala, and Marari beaches.

Travel Information

  • Best Time: September – March
  • Nearest Airport: Cochin International Airport (COK)
  • Top Hotels: Le Meridien Kochi, Taj Malabar Resort, Kumarakom Lake Resort
  • Local Transport: Taxis,Ferries, Auto-rickshaws
