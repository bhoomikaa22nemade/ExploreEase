About Kumarakom Lake Resort

Kumarakom Lake Resort, set on the serene banks of Vembanad Lake in Kerala, is one of India’s most luxurious heritage resorts. Spread across lush 25 acres, it combines traditional Kerala architecture with modern comforts. Famous for its infinity pool, Ayurvedic spa, and meandering pool villas, it is a perfect retreat for couples, families, and nature lovers.

Highlights

  • 🏡 Heritage villas with private pools
  • 🌊 Meandering Pool Villas connected by a winding pool
  • 🍽 Ettukettu restaurant & Vembanad Seafood Bar
  • 🕉 Ayurvedic spa treatments at Ayurmana
  • 🚤 Houseboat cruises & cultural performances
