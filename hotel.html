About Siolim House

Siolim House is a beautifully restored 350-year-old Portuguese-Goan mansion in North Goa. With just 9 exclusive rooms, it offers a tranquil escape from the crowds, combining heritage charm with modern comfort. The property is surrounded by lush gardens, a swimming pool, and peaceful courtyards. Guests can enjoy authentic Goan-Portuguese architecture and warm hospitality.

Highlights

  • 🏡 Heritage Portuguese-Goan mansion
  • 🌴 Lush tropical gardens
  • 🏊 Swimming pool & courtyards
  • 🍽 Traditional Goan & continental dining
  • 📍 Located in Siolim village, close to Morjim, Ashwem & Vagator beaches
