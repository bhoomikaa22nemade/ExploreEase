About Rambagh Palace

Once the residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur, Rambagh Palace is now a luxury Taj heritage hotel. Known as the "Jewel of Jaipur", it is a breathtaking palace with elegant Rajput architecture, lavish gardens, and unmatched royal hospitality. Guests experience the grandeur of Rajasthan’s regal past with world-class luxury.

Highlights

  • 👑 Former royal residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur
  • 🌺 Expansive Mughal gardens & courtyards
  • 🏊 Outdoor swimming pools & luxury spa
  • 🍽 Fine dining at Suvarna Mahal & Rajput Room
  • 🎭 Royal experiences – Elephant polo, cultural performances
